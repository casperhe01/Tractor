Tractor — rules

Tractor (also called “Shuang Kou”) is a four-player partnership game played with two full decks including jokers (108 cards). You (South) partner North against East and West. Each player gets 25 cards; 8 form the kitty. Point cards: 5 = 5, 10 = 10, K = 10 (200 in total).

Card order (example: playing 10s)

Trumps: Big joker, small joker, trump-suit 10, other 10s, then A K Q J 9 … 2 of the trump suit. Other suits: A K Q J 9 … 2. With “2 is permanent trump”, the 2s rank just below the 10s (trump-suit 2 above other 2s).

Pairs and tractors

A pair is two identical cards. A tractor is two or more pairs adjacent in rank and in the same suit, e.g. KKQQ, JJ99 (the 10s are trumps), 554433, or trump pairs such as small jokers + trump-suit 10s. Not tractors: 5533, two pairs of off-suit 10s, AA22.

Declaring trump

While the cards are dealt, the first player to show a card of the current level sets the trump suit. A second level card of the same suit locks it (self-protect). Another player can override by showing a pair of level cards in another suit. Optional rules: self-override (switch your own single to a pair of another suit), partner protect (your partner adds the second card), no-trump (show all four jokers). If nobody declares, the 3rd kitty card sets trump (skip jokers).

Playing

The declarer takes the kitty and buries 8 cards, then leads. You must follow suit with the same number of cards; if a pair is led you must play a pair if you have one; if a tractor is led you must play a tractor if you have one, else pairs. Several top cards of one suit may be led together (throw) — if any part can be beaten, the leader must play only the smallest beatable part. If you are void you may trump: your trumps must match the lead's pairs and tractors to win. Equal cards: the first played wins.

Scoring

Defenders collect points from tricks they win. If they win the last trick, the kitty points are multiplied: ×2 with a single, ×4 with a pair, ×8 with a tractor, ×16 with a three-pair tractor.

The option “Level up on taking over” adds one level whenever the defenders take over: 80 → +1, 120 → +2, 160 → +3.

Levels go 2, 3 … K, A, then no-trump (if the no-trump option is on). Levels you can't skip: J and A (if that option is on), 5/10/K (if that option is on), and always the no-trump level. At the no-trump level there is no trump suit and no declaring; only the jokers are trumps (2s are not trump here, even with “2 is permanent trump”). If the declarers win, the declarer's partner declares next; if the defenders take over, the next player after the old declarer declares. The first team to win a hand as declarers at the top level (no-trump, or A if no-trump is off) wins the match.

Tips (from the original help)

  • Bury few points unless you're sure the defenders can't win the last trick.
  • With the lead, play off-suit aces and tractors first.
  • Lead suits your partner is void in so they can trump points.
  • As declarer, bury a whole short suit so you can trump it later.
  • If you suspect points in the kitty, fight for the last trick.

拖拉机游戏规则

“拖拉机”，又名“双抠”，是由“升级”发展演化而来的扑克游戏。四人结对竞赛，抢分升级。使用两副牌共 108 张，每人 25 张，底牌 8 张。分牌：5 为 5 分，10 和 K 为 10 分，共 200 分。

牌的大小顺序（以打10为例）

主牌从大至小：大王，小王，主10，副10，A，K，Q，J，9，8，7，6，5，4，3，2。副牌：A，K，Q，J，9，8，7，6，5，4，3，2。2为常主时：大王，小王，主10，副10，主2，副2，A … 3。

拖拉机的构成

凡大小顺序相邻且花色相同的联对均构成拖拉机，如：KKQQ，JJ99，554433；主牌中凡大小顺序相邻联对均构成拖拉机，如：一对小王带一对主10，一对主10带一对副10，一对副10带一对主牌A。以下不是拖拉机：554，544，5533，两对副10，JJ1010，AA22。

亮牌规则

在发牌过程中，第一次亮出的10的花色作为主牌花色。自保：再亮出同花色的另一张10。反主：另一家亮出另一花色的一对10。自反：亮出一张10后又亮出另一花色的一对10（可选）。对家保：对家亮出该花色的第二张10（可选）。反无主：同时亮出四张王（可选）。若无人亮牌，以底牌第三张的花色为主（遇王顺延）。

打牌规则

同等大小的牌以先出者为大。同门花色的大牌可以联出（甩牌），若并非都是大牌，则必须出其中最小的牌。首家出对牌时，有对牌必须出对牌；首家出拖拉机时，有拖拉机必须出拖拉机，无拖拉机必须出对牌。无此门花色时可出主牌（毙），毙牌的拖拉机和对牌数目不得少于首家。

抠底与升级

以单张抠底底分乘二，对牌乘四，拖拉机乘八，三拖乘十六。闲家得0分庄家升三级；小于40分升二级；小于80分升一级。闲家得分大于等于80分且小于120分时，闲家上台；大于等于120分且小于160分时，闲家上台且升一级；大于等于160分且小于200分时，闲家上台且升二级，其余类推。（选项「上台即升级」：闲家上台时多升一级，即80分升一级，120分升两级，160分升三级。）级别顺序：2、3 … K、A，然后是无主（允许反无主时）。不可跳过的级别：J、A（选「A，J必打」时）、5、10、K（选「5，10，K必打」时），以及无主（打A后，若允许打无主，则必打）。打无主时没有主花色，也不亮主，只有大小王是主牌（即使选了「2为常主」，2也不是主牌）。庄家升级时由其对家坐庄；闲家上台时由原庄家的下家坐庄。在最高一级（无主，或不允许无主时的A）做庄并守住的一方获胜。

策略与技巧

  • 庄家扣底牌时少扣或不扣分。
  • 获得出牌权后，应先出副牌的A、拖拉机等大牌。
  • 出对家已出完的副牌，让同伴毙牌得分。
  • 扣光一门较短的副牌，以便毙牌。
  • 推测庄家在底牌扣了分时，尽量抠底。
